# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless
of experience, identity, background, appearance, disability, nationality, or
belief. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks and intimidation are
not.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not a person's worth or motives.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Respect privacy, licenses, attribution, and ownership of user assets.
- Avoid sexualized content, harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination, and
  sustained disruption.
- Stop behavior when a participant or moderator asks you to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when behavior threatens the community or project. Decisions should
be proportionate, documented privately where possible, and protect reporters
from retaliation.

The project does not yet have a configured private conduct contact. Until one is
published, do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.
This missing channel is a release blocker tracked by governance and CI.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
