# ADR 0005: MIT scope and third-party exclusions

- Status: Accepted by maintainer decision
- Date: 2026-07-22

## Decision

Original B2U/MiGR project code and documentation are offered under MIT with
`Copyright (c) 2026 B2U contributors`. Adding that license does not relicense
third-party models, textures, archives, packages, fixtures, or other assets.

## Consequences

Notices and the asset audit record separate terms. Release archives use code-only
allowlists and exclude unreviewed/restricted asset types and verification output.
Public release remains blocked until maintainers complete a rights review.
